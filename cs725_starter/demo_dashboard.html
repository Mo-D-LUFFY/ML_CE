How warning decisions work

BLOCKED Changing frozen history (age, education, marital status, recorded capital gain), exceeding work-hour limits, or changing occupation without explicit career access.

CAUTION A proposal obeys the hard policy, but would add more than 10 weekly hours or needs an unverified occupation change.

PASS No rule in this limited policy flagged the candidate. This is not certification that it will work in the real world.

Warning system = transparent rules, not a separately trained machine-learning model. No causal claim is made about actual income.
